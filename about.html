About Us

AromaLuxe is a gifting and lifestyle brand focused on creating beautiful, meaningful and memorable gifting experiences. Every gift should communicate emotions, love and appreciation. From handcrafted candles to personalized hampers, every creation is designed to make special occasions more memorable.

Collections include handmade candles, customized gift hampers, decorative gifts, bouquets, fragrances, chocolates, accessories, and festive gifting. AromaLuxe serves individuals, businesses, event organizers, and bulk buyers.
